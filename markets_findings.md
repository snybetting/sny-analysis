I bet on 2 markets mainly: corners and cards. The split in the sample is 1,427 card bets, and 1,173 corner bets. All-time ROI is effectively identical between the two: +10.48% and +10.15%.

By season, the ROI diverges pretty hard. Cards went from +15.06% to +8.27%, and corners went from +20.42% to +1.24%.

CLV didn't diverge. Cards went from +12.13% to +8.86% and corners went from +12.69% to +7.9%. Both markets lost roughly 4 points of closing line value and both sit near +8% now.

Conclusion: corners' +1.24% ROI looks like a dead market which i should drop, but it isn't - the CLV says i was still beating Pinnacle's close by nearly 8%. Both markets decayed by about the same amount, the difference in returns is variance. No reason to drop corners.

Caveats: CLV subsets are 281 and 308 bets, so the numbers aren't precise, and the 7.90 vs 8.86 gap hasn't been tested for significance.

My prior prediction was that corners' CLV would hold up in the 2nd season, and it did.
